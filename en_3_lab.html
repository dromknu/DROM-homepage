About the Lab

The Design & Reliability of Off-road Machinery Laboratory (DROM Lab) centers on the design, analysis, and reliability assessment of machinery operated off the road — tractors, combines, and heavy construction equipment. We integrate gear & transmission dynamics, precision design of PD pumps & flow meters, and physics-informed AI sensorless fluid control, so that lab theory reaches real products and the agricultural and industrial field. The accumulated results are commercialized as the core technology of EVER Co., Ltd., a KNU Holdings subsidiary.

Research Areas

01

Off-road Machinery Design

Precision design of tractor/combine/heavy-equipment drivetrains, MBD and structural analysis.

02

Reliability & Life Assessment

Accelerated-life, corrosion, durability testing and load-measurement-based reliability with robust design.

03

Gear & Transmission Dynamics

Dynamic factor & transmission-error prediction, planetary transmissions, gearbox churning loss.

04

PD Pump & Flow Metering

Leakage modeling and optimal design of rotary-clap, oval-gear, lobe, and vane pumps/meters.

05

Sensorless Fluid Control

±1% volumetric dosing using pump drive data and AI virtual sensors.

06

Physics-informed AI · Smart Ag

Physics + AI hybrid modeling for precision spraying and smart-farm automation.
